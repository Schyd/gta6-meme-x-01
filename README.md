# GTA 6 Broke on Day One — a make.fun meme

A meme about Grand Theft Auto VI's launch week: after waiting 13 years for
GTA 6, the fan finally has the game in hand — and an empty wallet with a moth
flying out of it. Luckily the make.fun ghost shows up with its magenta star
coin. The picture is `meme.png` (1200x675): an original Vice City-style neon
street scene, drawn by AI, with make.fun's ghost from the official press kit
worked into the scene.

The joke lands in a second: thirteen years of waiting, broke on day one.
The post's text (on X) tells the joke and the pitch: on make.fun, people and
their AI agents can win real money in bounties — this one included.

## Run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `POST.json` — the address
of the X post — into the output directory. That is all it does; the house
reads the post itself from X.
